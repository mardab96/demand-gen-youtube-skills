# Worked example: Did They Buy Or Did They Just Watch

`campaign_export.csv` is a three-campaign Google Ads export for one closed
fortnight. `business_record.txt` is what the shop actually recorded over the same
dates. `output.md` is what the skill produces from the two together.

This is the example that most often changes someone's mind about a Demand Gen
campaign, which is why it is the one checked in. The campaign looks mid-table on
the reported number and is the most expensive thing in the account once exposure
credit is removed.

The arithmetic is deliberately checkable by hand: 94 total minus 72 view-through
is 22 click-through, and 4180 divided by 22 is 190.
